# Checks and Conditions — owner checks

Part of the combined checklist for Phases 4.5.31 and 4.5.32; the published doc is linked from the build
ledger once both are merged and live. Each check is Pass, Fail or Not run; note the device.

Before you start: refresh the live app, use a copy of a character, tap **Manage**, and add the entry named
in each row from the Disadvantages (or Advantages) picker.

| # | Add | Do | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Brash** | tap **Check (TN 25)** on its row, then roll | a Willpower roll against TN 25; the preview and result list "Brash: Honor Rank" adding your Honor Rank; the result says whether you keep your temper |
| C2 | **Can't Lie**, **Overconfident**, **Soft-Hearted** | tap each row's **Check (TN 20)** | Willpower against TN 20 (Overconfident rolls Perception) |
| C3 | **Contrary** | change its TN box to 25, tap **Check** | "Contrary — Willpower vs TN 25" |
| C4 | **Epilepsy** | tap **Avoid (TN 15)**, then **End seizure (TN 10)** | two Willpower rolls, TN 15 then TN 10 |
| C5 | **Rumormonger** | tap **Check** with the box empty; then type 3 and tap it again | first a notice asking for the Glory Rank; then Willpower against TN 15 |
| C6 | **Lame** | roll an Agility Trait, then Strength | an unticked "Lame: Uses your legs: TN +10" box on Agility only; ticking it takes 10 off the total |
| C7 | **Missing Limb** | choose Left arm on its row, then roll anything (not damage) | an unticked "Uses your left arm: TN +10" box |
| C8 | **Disbeliever** | roll Courtier, then Lore | an unticked "A shugenja or monk is involved: TN +5" box on Courtier only |
| C9 | **Lost Love** | turn on **Reminded of your loss**, roll anything; then tap **Spend a Void Point** | −5 on every roll while on; the button spends one Void Point and turns the switch off |
| C10 | **Soft-Hearted** | turn on **Wracked with guilt**, roll anything | −10 on every roll while on |
| C11 | **Blind** | look at Armor TN (and Quick Access); then make a bow attack and a sword attack | Armor TN base is Reflexes + 5 (armor still adds); −3k3 on the bow, −1k1 on the sword |
| C12 | a Shugenja character without **Ishiken-Do** | open the Techniques picker | the Void spells are listed greyed, "— needs Ishiken-Do"; add Ishiken-Do and they become choosable (still padlocked until you have the scroll) |
| C13 | the same, with a Void spell already on the list | look at its row; tap its ✕; tap Cast | the ✕ report says "Needs Ishiken-Do"; Cast still works |
| C14 | a non-Shugenja character | open the Advantages picker | Ishiken-Do greyed, "— Shugenja only" |
| C15 | Lost Love on, Missing Limb's limb chosen; save, reload | look at the rows | the switch and the limb are as you left them |
| C16 | Any of them, then tap **Done** | the buttons and switches | still work in Play mode; Missing Limb's limb is locked |
| C17 | iPhone, portrait | the Contrary, Rumormonger, Lost Love and Missing Limb rows | nothing cut off or off the screen |
