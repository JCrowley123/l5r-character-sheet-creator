# Screen focus from the app bar — manual checks (desktop with a keyboard)

| # | Steps | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | In the app bar, click **Search**, then press **Escape**. | Search closes and the sheet shows. |
| A2 | Open Search → Spells, open any spell, click **Sheet** in the app bar, then **Search** again. Press **Escape** twice. | The first Escape closes the spell (its result is highlighted); the second closes Search. |
| A3 | In the app bar, click **Characters**, then press **Escape**. | The Characters list closes. |
