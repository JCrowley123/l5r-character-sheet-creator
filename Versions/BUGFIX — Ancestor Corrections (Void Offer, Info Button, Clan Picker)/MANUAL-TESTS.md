# Ancestor Corrections — iPhone check

About five minutes, once the fix is on the site you test. Use throwaway characters (Characters →
Create New Character, type a name, then **Exit** and **Leave** in the wizard).

## 1. Seppun's gift and the Void offer (check 4.23)

1. New character. On **Clan & School** pick Clan **Imperial**, Family **Seppun**, tap **Apply
   Family**, and choose the Ancestor **Seppun**. Check there is at least one Void Point.
2. On **Skills**, add **Battle** and roll it. Before ticking anything, the preview offers **+1k1**
   under "Spend a Void Point on this roll".
3. Tick **Seppun's gift**. **Check:** the Void section disappears; no "Make an Unskilled roll
   Skilled". Untick it: **+1k1** comes back.
4. Tick the gift again and roll. **Check:** one more die rolled and kept than without it, and your
   Void Points have not changed.

## 2. The Ancestor's i (check 4.1)

1. On **Clan & School**, look at the round **i** beside "Ancestor optional".
2. **Check:** it matches the Advantages' i (for example Dark Paragon's): the same size, a dark ring
   and an upright i, not the small gold italic one. Tap it: the Ancestor rules open.

## 3. The Ancestor list follows the Clan picker

1. New character. On **Clan & School**, without applying a Family, pick Clan **Imperial**, then
   open the Ancestor picker. **Check:** "Imperial families" comes first.
2. Pick Clan **Ronin**: "Ronin" comes first. Pick **Minor Clan**, then **Mantis**: "Mantis Clan".

Report anything that differs, with a screenshot.
