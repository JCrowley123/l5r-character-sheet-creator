# Phase 4.8 Ancestors — the gift audit (30 September 2026)

The owner's iPhone check of Kakita (30 September, feedback points 6 to 8) set a rule for how every
Ancestor's gift reaches a roll, and asked for an audit of every Ancestor against it, including a
check for Ancestors missing from the books supplied. This is that audit. Every description is in
this project's own words, with its page; nothing is quoted.

## The rule (the owner's words, summarised)

1. A gift that **changes the dice after the roll** (a re-roll, an extra die) is offered **after the
   roll**, in the result, like Luck. A player cannot know before rolling whether they will want it:
   a first roll of 50 is not one to re-roll.
2. A gift that **adds dice but costs something** (a Void Point, a once-a-session use) is **offered
   in the roll preview**, and the player chooses whether to take it.
3. A gift that is a **free, straight modifier** is **applied automatically** and **shown in the roll
   preview**.

Applying it to 54 Ancestors needed three more labels. None of them bends the rule:

- **With a Void Point**: the gift improves a Void Point you spend (Bayushi's +2k2, Kaiu's and Soshi
  Saibankan's +3k1). The Void Point is the choice and the cost, so it is ticked where it always
  is, in the preview's "Spend a Void Point" section, and the gift then applies by itself.
- **Tick it when you roll**: a free gift that depends on something the sheet cannot see (the roll
  is to detect a lie, the opponent is male, the spell is a Wards spell the sheet does not list).
  Offered in the preview; the player ticks it when it is true.
- **Shown when you roll**: a Free Raise, or Raises without limit. The sheet has no Raise mechanic
  (Phase 4.5's rule for Friend of the Elements), so the preview prints it and moves no dice.

Everything else is a **Reminder** on the card: narrative gifts, and effects on systems the sheet
does not model (Reduction against fire, Magic Resistance against maho, spells learned per Rank).

**How costs are taken.** A Void Point is spent by the sheet when you roll, through the same rules
as the Void card (none left, or one already spent this combat Round, and the sheet says so and
offers to roll without the gift). A once-a-session use is marked when you roll, with a **Reset
session** button on the card, as Luck and Dark Paragon have. Two costs are the player's to record,
because the sheet cannot take them honestly: **a point of Taint** (Hida Atarasi; the sheet keeps
Taint as a Rank, not in points) and **a spell slot** (Chuda Bikimi; the sheet would have to choose
the Element for you). The sheet asks, and says what to record.

## Missing Ancestors: the check

**From the books supplied, nothing is missing.**

- **Core Rulebook**: 18 Ancestors on pp. 242–244, all in the first release.
- **The Great Clans**: the index lists eight "New … Ancestors" sections (pp. 42, 104, 140, 170, 202,
  230, 260, 283); all eight were photographed, 16 Ancestors in all. **There is no Crane section**:
  the book adds no Crane Ancestor, so the Crane keep Doji and Kakita.
- **Secrets of the Empire**: the index recorded pp. 243–245 and eight entries; the photographs
  show pp. 243–247 and **20** Ancestors (the index's entry pattern missed the Imperial, Ronin and
  Brotherhood ones, and pages 246–247). Page 248 begins the School Index, so the section ends there.

**Groups with no Ancestor in these three books** (none is an error; each is simply not printed):
the Crane (in The Great Clans), the Falcon Clan, the Snake Clan (the Chuda's Ancestor, Chuda
Bikimi, is printed as a Spider), the Hantei, Toturi and Nasu Imperial families, and the Centipede
and Wasp Clans, which the sheet does not list (Moshi Azami covers the Centipede before the twelfth
century). The Fox Clan is covered by Osusuki & Akomachi (Kitsune blood).

**Not checked, and why:**

- **The wiki cross-check could not run.** This session's network policy refused both Ancestor
  pages (magicalsamurai.wikidot.com and lasthaiku.wikidot.com), as it did for the first release.
- **Other books.** The index finds the word "ancestor" in eleven more books (the five Elemental
  books, Emerald Empire, Enemies of the Empire, Imperial Histories 1 and 2, Naishou Province,
  Strongholds of the Empire, Sword and Fan), but none has a section of Ancestor Advantages among
  its headings: Emerald Empire's "Morning Prayer to the Ancestors" (p. 38) is a prayer, and
  Enemies of the Empire's "Yomi, the Realm of Blessed Ancestors" (p. 243) describes the realm. That
  page is the one worth a look. Checking any of them needs the pages.

## Readings the audit had to make

- **Seppun and Komori Iongi** both give, in effect, one free Void Point's worth once a session.
  The sheet caps Void Points at the Void Ring, so an "extra" point cannot sit in the tracker;
  both are offered in the preview as +1k1 without spending a Void Point, and the card's **Use it
  now** marks the session when the free point goes on something else (Armor TN, damage). Iongi's
  is a Void Point, so in combat it obeys one-a-Round; Seppun's is not spent, so it does not.
- **Toku** is Luck's re-roll (keep the higher), once a session, beside your own Luck.
- **Sun Tao's** extra die is added to the kept dice you chose, not re-sorted into the best set;
  with a Target Number shown, the sheet refuses it on a roll that already succeeded.
- **Kakita's** second roll is the first roll's pool plus 1k1, with every modifier it had; the
  better total is kept.
- **Miyuko** (+1 School Rank) and **Iuchi** (no Deficiency) change the Casting Roll's dice
  automatically; which spells the Spells tab lets you cast still follows the School Rank, so the
  card says so.
- **Resisting** (Hitomi, Reichin, Yamatsu) is offered on Willpower rolls, Earth Ring rolls and
  manual rolls, where 4th Edition resists Fear, Temptation and control.
- **Loyalty checks that go beyond the Clan** (Kitsu, Kitsune, Utaku and Iuchi blood; tattooed
  orders; Brotherhood devotions and sects; true ronin; women only) are warnings on the card, never
  blocks, as the Honor and Taint demands already are. The sheet has no gender field, so Otaku's
  is a note.
- **Chuda Bikimi** does not return: the first loss of his favour is final (p. 283).
