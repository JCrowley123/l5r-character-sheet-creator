# Phase 4.8 Ancestors — iPhone check

About ten minutes, on the branch preview. Start a **new character** for each part, so nothing
from an earlier test is carried over.

## 1. The card on the sheet

1. Open **Clan & School**. Under Clan, Family & School there is now an **Ancestor** block marked
   *optional*, with a picker and a round **i** button.
2. Choose Clan **Crab**, Family **Hida**, and press **Apply Family**.
3. Open the Ancestor picker. **Hida** and **Kuni** are offered, then **Hida Atarasi** and **Kuni
   Yori** under "Spider — only with your GM's permission". Every other Clan's Ancestors are listed
   greyed out.
4. Choose **Hida**. A card appears: Crab · 14 XP · Core Rulebook p. 242, the gifts with a tag on
   each (Automatic, Tick it when you roll, Reminder), and the demands.
5. Tap **i**. The shared Ancestor rules (Loyalty, Piety, Jealousy, Demands) and the Spider rule
   open in the information window.

**Look for:** nothing cut off or overlapping at phone width; the tags wrap neatly.

## 2. Cost and dice

1. Open **Identity**. The Experience breakdown shows **Ancestor: +14**, and Experience spent is 14
   higher than before.
2. Add a **Katana** on **Combat**. Its damage is one rolled die higher than without Hida (for
   example 7k2 instead of 6k2 with Strength 3). Roll the damage: the result says
   "Ancestor (Hida): +1k0 damage".

## 3. The favour badge

1. Back on **Clan & School**, press **Lost ancestor's favour** and confirm. The card fades, the
   badge fills in, and the picker is replaced by a line saying no other Ancestor can take
   Hida's place.
2. The Katana's damage drops back by one die; Experience spent does **not** change.
3. Press the badge again and confirm **Favour returned**. The gifts come back and the card says a
   second loss is final.
4. Press it once more and confirm **Lost for good**. The badge greys out and can no longer be
   pressed.

## 4. A Spider Ancestor

On a new Crab character, choose **Kuni Yori**. A question asks whether your GM agrees. **Cancel**
leaves no Ancestor; **My GM agrees** takes him, and the card says "Chosen with your GM's
permission".

## 5. Play mode

With an Ancestor chosen, press **Done** (Play mode). The picker shows its choice as plain text
and cannot be changed; the badge and the **i** button still work. Press **Manage** to edit again.

## 6. The wizard

Start a new character with the wizard. On the **Family** screen, below the Families, there is an
**Ancestor (optional)** section with a "No Ancestor" card and your Clan's Ancestors (plus the two
Spider ones). Pick one: its details appear below. On **Review**, an **Ancestor** line follows
Family.

## 7. A roll with a declared gift

On a new **Mantis** character (Minor Clan → Mantis, Family Yoritomo, Apply Family), choose
**Gusai**. Roll **Stealth** from the Skills tab (add it first). The roll preview offers
"Hiding a weapon on your person — +3k3"; ticking it adds three rolled and three kept dice.

Report anything that looks wrong, reads badly, or does not fit the screen.
