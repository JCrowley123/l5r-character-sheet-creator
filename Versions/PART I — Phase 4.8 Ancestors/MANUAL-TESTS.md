# Phase 4.8 Ancestors — iPhone check

About twenty minutes, on the branch preview. Start a **new character** for each part, so nothing
from an earlier test is carried over. Parts 5 to 9 are new in the second release (your feedback on
Kakita, and the two new books). The Manage button fix has its own short check, in
`BUGFIX — Manage Button Clipping/MANUAL-TESTS.md`.

## 1. The card on the sheet

1. Open **Clan & School**. Under Clan, Family & School there is an **Ancestor** block marked
   *optional*, with a picker and a round **i** button.
2. **The i button** looks like the circled **i** on the Advantages: the same size, gold ring and
   italic *i*. Compare it with one on the Advantages tab (your point 1).
3. Choose Clan **Crab**, Family **Hida**, and press **Apply Family**.
4. Open the Ancestor picker. Under "Crab Clan": **Hida**, **Kuni**, **Hiruma** and **Kaiu**; then
   **Hida Atarasi**, **Kuni Yori**, **Chuda Bikimi** and **Yogo Junzo** under "Spider — only with
   your GM's permission". Every other Ancestor is listed greyed out.
5. Choose **Hida**. A card appears: Crab · 14 XP · Core Rulebook p. 242, the gifts with a tag
   on each (Automatic, Reminder), and the demands.
6. Tap **i**. The shared Ancestor rules (Loyalty, Piety, Jealousy, Demands), then the Spider, ronin
   and Brotherhood rules, open in the information window.

**Look for:** nothing cut off or overlapping at phone width; the tags wrap neatly.

## 2. Cost and dice

1. Open **Identity**. The Experience breakdown shows **Ancestor: +14**, and Experience spent is 14
   higher than before.
2. Add a **Katana** on **Combat**. Its damage is one rolled die higher than without Hida (for
   example 7k2 instead of 6k2 with Strength 3). Roll the damage: the result says
   "Ancestor (Hida): +1k0 damage".

## 3. The favour badge

1. Back on **Clan & School**, press **Lost ancestor's favour** and confirm. The card fades, the
   badge fills in, and the picker is replaced by a line saying no other Ancestor can take
   Hida's place.
2. The Katana's damage drops back by one die; Experience spent does **not** change.
3. Press the badge again and confirm **Favour returned**. The gifts come back and the card says a
   second loss is final.
4. Press it once more and confirm **Lost for good**. The badge greys out and can no longer be
   pressed.

## 4. A Spider Ancestor

On a new Crab character, choose **Kuni Yori**. A question asks whether your GM agrees. **Cancel**
leaves no Ancestor; **My GM agrees** takes him, and the card says "Chosen with your GM's
permission".

## 5. Kakita: the re-roll comes after the roll (your points 6 and 7)

1. New character: Clan **Crane**, Family **Kakita**, Apply Family; choose **Kakita**. Make sure the
   character has at least one Void Point.
2. Add **Iaijutsu** on Skills and roll it. The roll preview offers **no** Kakita tick.
3. Under the result: "Kakita — spend a Void Point to re-roll with +1k1, and keep the better
   result", with a **Re-roll with Kakita (a Void Point)** button.
4. Press it. One Void Point is spent, and a line says the two totals and which roll is kept.
5. Roll **Kenjutsu** (add it first): no Kakita offer under that result.

## 6. A gift that costs a Void Point, and one that improves a Void Point

1. **Kuni** (chosen when you roll). New character: Crab, Family **Kuni**, the **Kuni Shugenja**
   School (the wizard is the quickest way to get a spell). With at least one Void Point, cast any
   spell. The preview offers "Kuni's gift — +0k… (a Void Point; once a session)". Tick it and press
   **Roll**: one Void Point is spent, and the card now says **Kuni's gift: used this session (0/1)**
   with a **Reset session** button. Cast again: the tick is still listed, with a note that this
   session's use is already spent; ticking it and pressing **Roll** asks whether to roll without
   it. Press **Reset session**: the note says again that the sheet takes it when you roll.
2. **Bayushi** (with a Void Point). New character: Clan **Scorpion**, Family **Bayushi**, School
   **Bayushi Courtier**, Apply School; choose **Bayushi**. Roll one of the School's Skills: the
   preview already shows Bayushi's +1k0. Tick **Spend a Void Point** (+1k1): the preview adds
   Bayushi's +1k1 as well, with no second tick.

## 7. Once a session without a Void Point: Seppun

1. New character: Clan **Imperial**, Family **Seppun**; choose **Seppun**. The card shows
   **Seppun's free Void Point: ready this session (1/1)** with **Use it now** and **Reset session**.
2. Roll any Skill. The preview offers "Seppun's gift: a Void Point's +1k1 without spending one".
   Tick it and press **Roll**: your Void Points do **not** change, and the card now says used (0/1).
3. Press **Reset session**, then **Use it now**: it asks first, then marks the session used.

## 8. After any roll: Toku's Luck

New character: Clan **Minor Clan** → **Monkey**, Family **Toku**; choose **Toku**. Roll anything:
under the result, "Toku — a Rank of Luck: re-roll the whole roll and keep the higher result", with
**Spend Toku's Luck (1/1)**. Press it: a line says both totals and which is kept. On the next roll
the button reads (0/1) and cannot be pressed, until **Reset session** on the card.

## 9. The new factions in the picker

Each on a new character, open the Ancestor picker:

- Clan **Imperial**: "Imperial families" lists **Otomo**, **Seppun** and **Miya**.
- Clan **Ronin**: "Ronin" lists **Sun Tao**, **Chiroru** and **Miyuko**.
- **Minor Clan** → **Badger**: "Badger Clan" lists **Ichiro Fureheshu**.
- **Minor Clan** → **Mantis**: "Mantis Clan" lists **Kaimetsu-Uo**, **Gusai**, **Moshi Azami** and
  **Osusuki & Akomachi**. **Minor Clan** → **Fox**: "Fox Clan" lists **Osusuki & Akomachi** too.
- A **Brotherhood** monk School: "Brotherhood of Shinsei" lists **Basso**, **Sakura**, **Mizumoto**
  and **Togashi Kaze**.

## 10. Play mode

With an Ancestor chosen, press **Done** (Play mode). The picker shows its choice as plain text
and cannot be changed; the badge, the **i** button and the session buttons still work. Press
**Manage** to edit again.

## 11. The wizard

Start a new character with the wizard. On the **Family** screen, below the Families, there is an
**Ancestor (optional)** section with a "No Ancestor" card, your Clan's Ancestors, then the Spider
ones. Pick one: its details appear below. On **Review**, an **Ancestor** line follows School.

## 12. A roll with a ticked gift

On a new **Mantis** character (Minor Clan → Mantis, Family Yoritomo, Apply Family), choose **Gusai**. Roll
**Stealth** from the Skills tab (add it first). The roll preview offers "Hiding a weapon on your
person — +3k3"; ticking it adds three rolled and three kept dice.

Report anything that looks wrong, reads badly, or does not fit the screen.
