# Step 3b · Spells when a School Rank advances
3 November 2026. Read from the owner’s fourth-edition books. References below use **printed page numbers**. The catalogue used for the cross-check is the one in live Release 8b.1.

## Osamu’s next advancement
When Kitsu Shugenja advances from School Rank 1 to 2, Osamu normally learns **three new spells**. He can choose any mix of legal Elements within the Mastery he can cast at the new rank. There is no repeat of the starting three Water, two Air and one Earth allotment. His later reward and any unfinished creation choices are separate. A skipped rank choice belongs to its original rank.

## The rule and exceptions
- **Ordinary advancement.** The general Shugenja rule gives three spells in place of a new Technique. Use the rank earned in the advancing School, including the appropriate elemental affinity or deficiency when determining available Mastery. The core example of changing Schools freezes the former School. Do not give both Schools a grant for one Insight increase. A character’s starting package already covers the initial rank at creation. Core Rulebook pp.104–105, 164.
- **Alternate Paths.** The first Path taken by a shugenja counts towards the basic School for learning and casting and gives one spell in place of the normal three. Later Paths give no spells and do not increase effective basic School Rank for this purpose. The example is a Kitsu character. Use actual Path history, including across held Schools. Core Rulebook p.246.
- **Advanced Schools.** A rank allocated to an Advanced School earns its printed Technique and freezes advancement in the prior basic School. Increased casting ability or daily spell slots printed in a Technique do not by themselves give another basic-School grant. Core Rulebook pp.245, 248–249. Advanced Technique effects follow the existing Phase 4.7 reference policy.
- **Spell access.** Scrolls and memorisation are separate. A rank pick supplies the learned spell and its scroll. Memorisation is a subsequent player choice. Void spells require Ishiken-do. Core Rulebook pp.151, 164. A starting allotment that permits particular spells or spell families does not by itself grant continuing access to restricted magic.
- **Other rules encountered.** The Isawa *ancestor* grants an extra spell per Shugenja School Rank while its benefit applies (The Great Clans p.202). Membership of the Isawa School alone does not grant that bonus. The self-taught ronin has no automatic advancement spells and the single-sensei ronin depends on the relationship with the teacher (Enemies of the Empire p.205). Neither ronin School is in the current character builder. Creature magic, ancestor benefits and individual Technique effects are distinct from a basic School’s ordinary allotment.

The two Chuda entries are different Schools. The Crab, Crane, Dragon, Lion, Mantis, Phoenix, Scorpion, Unicorn, Minor Clan and Imperial entries use their own profiles. Yogo’s Ward treatment and Chuda’s blood magic need separate eligibility checks from an ordinary elemental affinity. For advancement the specific test is the Mastery the character could cast at the new rank. For Yogo, apply the non-Void Ward affinity (including the School’s named substitutes) and the Travel or Craft deficiency. At Rank 2 this admits Bonds of Ningen-Do and excludes Call Upon the Wind. The Spider Chuda may learn Maho on advancement and can cast it without the usual Mastery ceiling. The Snake School does not inherit that privilege from the shared name. Core p.267 also suggests an optional GM limit on Maho learning in a Spider campaign. The picker uses the default rule. Great Clans p.229 and Core pp.213, 267. The app’s supported advancement Paths and the distinct starting-choice record must be respected before writing a grant.

## Owner’s ruling on older saves
Track future ranks automatically. In Manage the player can report exactly how many choices are still missing from each older rank. Opening the character, dismissing the review or choosing Later never grants a past spell. The one-time starting-spell review in Release 8b.1 remains separate.
