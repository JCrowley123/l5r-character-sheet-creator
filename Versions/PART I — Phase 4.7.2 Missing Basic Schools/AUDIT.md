# Missing Basic Schools — source audit

Verified against the owner's *Imperial Histories* on 4 October 2026, printed pages 147 and 276–277. These are five-rank Basic Schools, not Advanced Schools. Their Techniques are source-cited reference text; conditional combat effects are not automated in this release.

- **Hiruma Scout [Bushi], Crab:** +1 Reflexes, Honor 4.5. Athletics, Hunting, Kenjutsu, Kyujutsu, Lore: Shadowlands, Stealth (Sneaking), and any one Bugei Skill. The entry prints no outfit; no package is invented. Rank 1 doubles the **duration** of food, water and jade rations for Hunting Rank people. Existing Alternate Paths that explicitly name Hiruma Scout now resolve to this School.
- **Yotsu Bushi School, Tiger Clan, Heroes of Rokugan:** +1 Agility, Honor 5.5. Commerce, Hunting, Kenjutsu, Kyujutsu, one of Lore: Gaijin or Lore: Shadowlands, Stealth (Sneaking), and one Low or Bugei Skill. The two Lore subjects are alternatives, not two free Skills or an Emphasis. Outfit: daisho, light or ashigaru armor, two other weapons, sturdy clothing, travelling pack and 4 koku. Tiger's Yotsu family grants Intelligence. The School name explicitly identifies the alternate timeline. Canonical Ronin Yotsu and the Sword of Yotsu Path are retained.

Both use existing School application, free Skill choices, Insight progression and character saves. A repeated Apply must replace prior grants without duplicating Skills or bonuses. The new Yotsu Rank 4 title is qualified for lookup safety, while the source's title remains identifiable.
