# Two Basic Schools — worked manual tests

Use a new disposable character in **Manage**, on Windows or iPhone. These are **Basic Schools on Clan & School**, not the Advanced School selector on Techniques. Use the current branch HTML; the hosted site updates after merge/deployment. A pass on one device is sufficient for these rules checks; iPhone layout remains separately recordable.

## 1. Normal case — Hiruma Scout

Choose **Crab**, Family **Hiruma**, and **Hiruma Scout [Bushi]**. Apply Family and School, or choose them in a new-character wizard.

- Starting Honor should be **4.5**.
- Family grants Agility +1; the School grants Reflexes +1. With ordinary starting Traits 2, those become **3** each.
- The School grants Rank 1 Athletics, Hunting, Kenjutsu, Kyujutsu, Lore: Shadowlands and Stealth, with **Sneaking** Emphasis free on Stealth.
- Choose **Defense** for the one additional Bugei Skill. It should be Rank 1 and marked as a School Skill.
- Rank 1 Technique is **Dance the Razor's Edge**. Its reference says rations last twice as long for Hunting Rank people. No starting outfit was printed in this School entry; agree equipment with the GM rather than expecting an invented automatic package.

Fringe: press Apply School again. Traits, Skills and Techniques must not duplicate or gain another free rank.

## 2. Progression and Path compatibility — Hiruma Scout

For this throwaway character, use Identity's **Other Insight Bonus** to reach total Insight Points **150, 175, 200, then 225**, one step at a time. Add only the difference between current points and the target.

At successive ranks, expect **Run Like the Wind**, **Veil of the Spirits**, **Harness the Wind**, and **Strike of the Stalker**. There should be five School Techniques at Rank 5, each with an Imperial Histories reference. Conditional effects remain text for the player to apply.

On a fresh Scout at the relevant rank, check that existing Paths naming Hiruma Scout can now be offered when their own requirements are met. For example, **The Falcon’s Strike** requires Kyujutsu 3 with Yumi Emphasis and replaces Rank 2. For Hiruma, its setting is after the Falcon join the Crab, early in the twelfth century. Do not expect a Path with unmet prerequisites to qualify merely because the School now exists.

## 3. Normal case — Yotsu and the Tiger Clan

Create another character. Choose **Minor Clan → Tiger → Yotsu**, then **Yotsu Bushi School (Heroes of Rokugan) [Bushi]**. This full School belongs to the alternate-history setting named in its title.

- Starting Honor is **5.5**.
- Family grants Intelligence +1; School grants Agility +1. With ordinary starting Traits 2, both become **3**.
- Fixed Rank 1 Skills: Commerce, Hunting, Kenjutsu, Kyujutsu and Stealth. Sneaking is the free Emphasis.
- In the wizard's first free choice, choose **Lore: Gaijin**. It must add a Rank 1 School Skill using Intelligence. It must not add both Gaijin and Shadowlands.
- For the other choice, choose **Defense** (Bugei) or **Sleight of Hand** (Low). You should finish with seven School Skills total.

Fringe: repeat with a fresh character and choose **Lore: Shadowlands** instead. The Lore picker must offer only those two subjects, not Lore: History or every Skill. Save and reopen: the chosen subject must remain a free School Skill.

## 4. Canonical Yotsu remains separate

On Clan & School, change the picker to **Ronin**. The existing Yotsu family remains available. The new five-rank School must not be mislabelled as a canonical Ronin School. The existing **Sword of Yotsu** Path and its **Shelter the Blameless** Technique must retain their original text.

For a Tiger Yotsu, increase total Insight Points to **225** as in test 2. Its five Techniques should be **The Tiger's Tread**, **The Tiger's Pounce**, **Rending Claws**, **Shelter the Blameless (Yotsu Bushi)** and **The Tiger's Fangs**. The qualifier prevents confusion with the Ronin Path; it is not an extra Technique.

## 5. Save, repeat, and layout

Save each character, close/reopen it, and check the School, free Skills, Emphasis and Technique count. Repeat Apply School once and confirm no duplicate grants. Try switching to a different Basic School on a disposable copy: old starting bonuses must be replaced rather than stacked.

On iPhone, check that the long Yotsu School name and both wizard choice boxes fit in portrait and landscape. Buttons must remain reachable. Record actual device results; automated narrow-screen checks are not an iPhone test.
