# Printed weapon columns — manual checks

Run on the live app after the merge, on a **copy** of a character (Manage → Save As a copy) that has the long Katana
from Cycle 1's W1: a Katana from the list, renamed **Katana of the Unbroken Line, heirloom blade of the Kakita dueling
house**. These repeat the two failed or flagged rows of the Cycle 1 checklist.

| # | Steps | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| W4 | iPhone: Characters → the copy's ⋯ → **Export to PDF**, then Share → Print (or Save to Files), and open the PDF. | On the weapons page the Weapon and Notes columns are the wide ones. The name breaks only between words ("Katana of the Unbroken / Line, heirloom blade …"). Skill, Attack, Damage and Size are just wide enough for "Kenjutsu", the two rolls and "Medium". The whole note is readable. |
| W2 | Computer: the same Export to PDF, save the PDF and open it. | No scrollbar under the Weapons table; "Kenjutsu" and "Medium" are whole; the name and note wrap between words. |
| W5 | iPhone and computer: close the print dialog and look at Combat → Weapons. | It looks as before: one-line fields, the same columns. |
| W6 | Optional: add a **Tetsubo** from the list and export again. | "Heavy Weapons" stays on one line; nothing is cut off. |
