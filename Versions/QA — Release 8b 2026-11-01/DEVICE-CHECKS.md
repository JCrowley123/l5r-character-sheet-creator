# Release 8b — device checks

Use a spare character or a saved copy for these 11 checks. [Open the live Release 8b app](https://l5r-character-sheet-creator.pages.dev/).

If you prefer a separate character list for testing, you can load your copy in the [verified Release 8b preview](https://2101de40.l5r-character-sheet-creator.pages.dev/).

Tick a device only after its result matches Pass. The [HTML checklist](DEVICE-CHECKS.html) has larger controls and space for notes.

## 1. Open the notes

Use a Kitsu character with an unfinished free Skill and starting scrolls. In Manage, open Identity and find Character check.

**Pass:** The two notes offer Choose School Skills and Choose School scrolls.

- [ ] iPhone
- [ ] Edge

## 2. Switch to Play

Return to Play and look at the same notes.

**Pass:** They are plain notes, with no picker buttons.

- [ ] iPhone
- [ ] Edge

## 3. Cancel a Skill choice

In Manage, open Choose School Skills and press Done without adding anything.

**Pass:** No Skill, XP or School choice changes. The note stays.

- [ ] iPhone
- [ ] Edge

## 4. Choose a free Skill

Open the Skill picker, choose an eligible new Skill and press Add.

**Pass:** It appears at Rank 1 with School ticked and no XP cost for that rank. The satisfied note disappears.

- [ ] iPhone
- [ ] Edge

## 5. Keep a paid Skill

On an older character, choose an eligible Skill already held above Rank 1. Then use Choose again in this picker.

**Pass:** No duplicate is added. Paid ranks remain, including after Choose again.

- [ ] iPhone
- [ ] Edge

## 6. Pick or leave each Element

Open School scrolls. Choose a spell, or use Leave [Element] for later.

**Pass:** Only appropriate choices are offered. The next available Element appears. Skipped choices remain in the note when you close.

- [ ] iPhone
- [ ] Edge

## 7. Add the given scrolls

If Sense, Commune and Summon are missing from the School choices, use the given-scroll button.

**Pass:** They are recorded as School choices and appear in Equipment. They are not automatically memorised for XP.

- [ ] iPhone
- [ ] Edge

## 8. Check a reward

Add a suitable scroll through ordinary Equipment. Look at the School note, then explicitly choose that same scroll in the School picker.

**Pass:** Equipment alone does not satisfy the School note. The explicit School choice does, without another copy.

- [ ] iPhone
- [ ] Edge

## 9. Save and reopen

Save, switch to another character, then return.

**Pass:** Completed choices stay completed. Unfinished choices stay available. No picker from the other character remains open.

- [ ] iPhone
- [ ] Edge

## 10. Finish and undo

Finish the remaining School choices. If testing undo, remove a choice through the School picker before closing.

**Pass:** Satisfied notes disappear. Undo reopens the relevant choice and preserves previously owned or memorised items.

- [ ] iPhone
- [ ] Edge

## 11. Phone and desktop controls

Use the pickers on iPhone and Edge. Scroll the dialog if needed; on Edge also press Escape.

**Pass:** Text and buttons fit, selectors remain usable, Done closes the dialog, and Escape closes it on Edge.

- [ ] iPhone
- [ ] Edge
