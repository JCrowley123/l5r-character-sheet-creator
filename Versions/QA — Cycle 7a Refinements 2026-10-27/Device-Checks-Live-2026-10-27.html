Cycle 7a refinements
Device checks

27 October 2026 · release d5c5efb · production checks

Test the live app. Reload it online first so the new release is available. These are fresh results for the deployed refinements; earlier device findings are preserved separately.

Before you start

  1. Use a copy of a character so test changes do not affect your campaign character.
  2. Record the browser and device in the notes. Test both laptop Edge and iPhone Safari.
  3. All results begin as Not run. Change each dropdown after completing its steps. Results are stored in this browser on this device; they are not shared between devices. Use Export findings to save a copy you can attach in this chat. Import findings restores a saved copy on another device.

1. Compact cards

  1. Open a disposable character’s Identity screen on laptop, then phone.
  2. Check that Honour, Glory, Status and Taint are together; each shows separate Rank and Points, with nine small pips on their own row.
  3. Check that cards have matching heights at each screen width and no decimal total is shown.
Laptop · EdgeiPhone · Safari

2. Point adjustment and Undo

  1. Use Management to set Honour Rank 6 and Points 5 on the disposable character.
  2. Enter 3 between minus and plus, then press minus. Expect Rank 6, Points 2 and the adjustment box back at 1.
  3. Use Undo. Expect Rank 6, Points 5 again. Try a blank, zero, negative number and decimal: each must explain the error and leave the character unchanged.
Laptop · EdgeiPhone · Safari

3. Glory and Status banked points

  1. Set Status Rank 5 and Points 9; return to Play.
  2. Add one point. Expect Rank 5, Points 10 and no filled pips. Add one again: Points 11 and one filled pip.
  3. In Management, use Recognition by your lord. Expect Rank 6 and Points 1. Check that recognition is unavailable in Play.
Laptop · EdgeiPhone · Safari

4. Taint and school information

  1. On Identity, find Taint beside the other three characteristics.
  2. Check that its growth check and school-related information are still available below the group.
  3. Adjust Taint by a positive whole number, then use Undo. Confirm the number and pips restore.
Laptop · EdgeiPhone · Safari

5. Campaign wounds

  1. Use a character with Earth 2 and no wound-modifying Advantages or Disadvantages. Open Management → Rings & Traits.
  2. Select ×2, then inspect Play → Combat. Expect cumulative thresholds 10, 14, 18, 22, 26, 30, 34, 38.
  3. Choose ×3: expect 10, 16, 22, 28, 34, 40, 46, 52. Choose custom 6: expect 10, 22, 34, 46, 58, 70, 82, 94.
  4. Try zero, a negative number, a decimal and blank custom input. Expect an explanation, no invalid change and only one active choice. Check wounds already taken are preserved. Save and reload to confirm the multiplier persists.
Laptop · EdgeiPhone · Safari

6. Named PDF save

  1. On Characters, choose Export to PDF for the test character.
  2. Choose a filename and prepare the PDF; then choose Save.
  3. On Edge, check the offered save location and filename. On iPhone Safari, check the available share/save-to-Files flow and filename. Browser support determines the native dialog.
  4. Cancel once and retry. Confirm the dialog remains usable and the resulting PDF belongs to the selected character.
Laptop · EdgeiPhone · Safari

7. Print from the sheet

  1. Open the character sheet, then its three-dot menu → Print.
  2. Check it opens the same PDF export dialog. Prepare and choose Open PDF to print.
  3. Use the PDF viewer’s Print action. Confirm the template sheet is printed, rather than a capture of the webpage. If a popup is blocked, confirm the app explains this.
Laptop · EdgeiPhone · Safari

8. PDF fields and rules

  1. Export a character with Etiquette and known school techniques.
  2. Check the Trait column uses readable names or abbreviations with its legend; check school technique text, Notes lines and Kata labels are clean.
  3. Confirm the generated Miscellaneous instructions are blank and the wound totals and formula match the character’s campaign setting.
Laptop · EdgeiPhone · Safari

9. Optional appendix and saved notes

  1. Export once with the details appendix unchecked, then once checked.
  2. Check the second version adds clearly titled ability descriptions. The first still contains actual character notes, custom abilities and entries that overflow the template.
  3. Check neither version loses text or creates unexplained blank pages.
Laptop · EdgeiPhone · Safari

10. Reload and everyday use

  1. Save the disposable character, reload the browser and open it again.
  2. Confirm Rank, Points and the wound multiplier persist, while the adjustment amount starts at 1.
  3. Repeat a normal skill roll, a wound change and a PDF export. Record any regression below.
Laptop · EdgeiPhone · Safari
