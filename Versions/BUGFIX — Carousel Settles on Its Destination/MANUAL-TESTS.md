# Carousel settles on its destination — manual checks (iPhone)

| # | Steps | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | On Rings & Traits, tap the **Background** tab. | The tab bar highlights Background at once and stays on it while the sheet slides; the sheet stops on Background. |
| C2 | Tap **Background**, and rotate the phone while the sheet is sliding. | The sheet ends on Background, not on a page it passed. |
| C3 | Reload the sheet, tap **Background** straight away, then tap Notes and type. | The text appears in Notes. |
| C4 | Swipe left and right between pages; use the arrows if shown. | Unchanged: each swipe or arrow moves one page. |
| C5 | Tap a far tab, and put a finger on the sheet while it slides. | The sheet follows the finger, as before. |
