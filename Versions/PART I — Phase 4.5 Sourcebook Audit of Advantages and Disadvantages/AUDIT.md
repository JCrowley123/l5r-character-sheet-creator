# Advantages and Disadvantages — Sourcebook Audit

> The repository copy of the audit published as the Claude Doc [Advantages and Disadvantages — Sourcebook Audit](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/485b7ec0-c6cd-4c63-b9e6-3e5c4ecf74a6), 2 October 2026. An audit only: nothing in the sheet was changed. Book text was read from a scratch folder and is never stored here; every summary is in our own words (owner's ruling, 30 September 2026).

Oct 2, 2026 · @James Crowley

**The sheet has every Advantage and Disadvantage in the Core Rulebook, but acts on only a third of them, and 65 entries from your supplements are missing.** It holds 139 entries; 44 have a picker and 89 only record their cost and text. 42 of the missing entries can be taken by the characters the sheet builds; the other 23 are for Naga or Nezumi only.

- **Pickers still needed: 25.** 9 for entries already in the sheet (Weakness, Bad Fortune and Missing Limb among them) and 16 for missing ones, plus 4 missing heirlooms and weapons that fit the existing Inheritance and Sacred Weapon pickers.
- **Could be automated with what the sheet already does: 94.** 60 of the 89 record-only entries and 34 of the 42 usable missing ones. Each is matched to a working example in the Automation section.
- **Cheapest single win: Clan and School prices.** 43 entries cost less (or more) for some Clans or Schools, and the sheet charges every one at the catalogue price. One shared step, like Sacred Weapon's Clan price, would fix them all.
- **Next cheapest: Crab Hands, Crafty, Sage and Sensation.** Each treats an untrained Skill family as Rank 1, exactly as Soul of Artistry already does.
- **Rulings needed:** Weakness (already pending), Student of the Past (no cost printed), Trials of the Imperial City (a printing error), and whether to add entries for Naga, Nezumi or the Emerald Empire's Station rules, which the sheet does not have.

## In the sheet now

**The sheet has 139 entries: 73 Advantages and 66 Disadvantages.** That is every Advantage and Disadvantage in the Core Rulebook (131), plus 8 from supplements. **44 open a picker or choice window** when added; 6 more are handled without one (the School controls, Kiho prices, the Blessing discount, the Servant reference and the Void Kiho ban). **The other 89 record their cost and description only**: the sheet applies none of their effects.

"Core p." is the Core Rulebook. XP is the catalogue's starting cost; several vary with a choice.

## Configuration

**44 entries already have a picker. 9 more already in the sheet would need one, and so would 16 of the 42 usable missing entries; 4 more missing ones fit as options in pickers the sheet already has.** Separately, **43 entries need no picker but cost less for some Clans or Schools**: the sheet could price them itself, as it already does for Sacred Weapon.

The last column names an entry that already works the same way, so each new picker copies a tested one.

## How this was checked

**Every count here was measured on 2 October 2026, against the live build and the text of your 16 books.** Nothing in the sheet was changed.

- **The sheet.** The live build (`main`, build `033a0bf2…`) was opened headlessly. Its two catalogues gave the 139 entries. Each entry's configuration was looked up with the sheet's own lookup functions, the ones the Advantage and Disadvantage lists use. To find what else acts on an entry, every source file was searched for its name, and each hit was read; hits that were only Technique or Path text were discarded.
- **The books.** The text of all 16 PDFs was extracted to a scratch folder, never to the repository. The search took every heading of the form “Name \[Type\] (Cost)”, in either order and over one or two lines, and every line saying “New Advantage” or “New Disadvantage”. The Core Rulebook's summary near its end (after p.380) repeats every entry, so it was left out. Each book page is the printed page.
- **Matching.** Names were compared ignoring case and apostrophe style. All 139 sheet entries matched exactly one book heading, and the Core Rulebook's 131 headings are all in the sheet.
- **The Element books.** The Advantages sections of the five Element books (Air, Earth, Fire, Water, Void) discuss entries already in the sheet; their genuinely new entries (Battle Healing, Reincarnated, the four Book of Void village Advantages, Uncentered) were found by the heading search.
- **Our own words.** Every summary paraphrases the mechanics, per your ruling of 30 September; nothing is quoted.
- **The 19 September audit.** Of the 23 missing configurations that audit found, 22 are now built. Weakness waits for your rulings, and the Seven Fortunes' Curse's Hotei branch is still open.

**Not covered.** Books you do not have (The Second City, for example) were not searched, and the fan wikis were not used. Each judgement of “needs a picker” or “could be automated” comes from the entry's one-paragraph rule; the full design of each build (its window, saved fields, checks and removal) is worked out when that build is approved, as your September prompt asked. Three points need your ruling: Student of the Past prints no cost or effect, Trials of the Imperial City repeats another entry's text, and Wanderer prints no type.
