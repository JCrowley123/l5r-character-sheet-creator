Release 8b.1 device checks

Import a fresh copy of Osamu into the Release 8b.1 preview. In Manage, open Identity → Character check.

Tick each device after you see the expected result. You can send the check number with any difference you find.

  1. Import a fresh copy. In Manage, open Identity → Character check. Look at Defense on the Skills page.

    Pass · There is no free Skill to choose. Defense is still ticked at Rank 3 and shows 5 XP.

  2. Open Review starting spells. Select Sense, then tap Later. Reopen the review.

    Pass · All nine existing spells are listed. Reopening shows the still-unconfirmed review with no selections.

  3. Select Sense, Commune and Summon, all three Water spells and both Air spells. Leave Jurojin’s Balm unselected. Tap Confirm starting spells.

    Pass · The ordinary picker offers one Earth choice. The given, Water and Air groups are complete.

  4. Pick a different Earth spell. Close the picker. Inspect Techniques and Equipment.

    Pass · The note clears. The eight confirmed spells each appear once. The two Earth spells are the new School spell and the original reward. Only the new one carries the School label.

  5. Save, open a different character, then return. Also try exporting and importing the reviewed character.

    Pass · The completed review stays complete. Defense, paid ranks, the reward and the new Earth choice survive.

  6. Open another fresh copy and switch to Play before reviewing. Return to Manage and try the review there.

    Pass · Play shows a plain note. The review action appears in Manage.
