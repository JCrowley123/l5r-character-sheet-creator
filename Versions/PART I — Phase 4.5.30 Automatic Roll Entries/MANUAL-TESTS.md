# Automatic Roll Entries — owner checks

Part of the combined checklist for Phases 4.5.29 and 4.5.30; the published doc is linked from the build
ledger once both are merged and live. Each check is Pass, Fail or Not run; note the device.

Before you start: refresh the live app, use a copy of a character, tap **Manage**. Each entry needs no
choice: add it, then roll, and the preview lists it by name.

| # | Add | Roll | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Silent** | Stealth (add the Skill) | +1k0: one more die rolled, "Silent" in the preview |
| A2 | **Prodigy** (with a School) | one of your School's Skills; then a Skill your School does not teach | +1k0 on the first; nothing on the second |
| A3 | **Voice** | Perform: Song, then Perform: Storytelling, then Perform: Dance | +1k1 on Song and Storytelling; nothing on Dance |
| A4 | **Bad Eyesight** | a bow attack; a Perception Trait roll; a sword attack | −1k1 on the first two; nothing on the sword |
| A5 | **Disturbing Countenance** | Courtier | "Disturbing Countenance … (TN +5)", 5 off the total |
| A6 | **Anachronism** | its row; then Artisan (any kind) and Courtier | the row says only returned spirits may take it; 5 off each roll |
| A7 | Disturbing Countenance and Anachronism together | Courtier | both listed: 10 off the total |
| A8 | Any of them, then tap **Done** | the same roll | still applied in Play mode |
| A9 | iPhone, portrait | Anachronism's row; a roll preview listing three entries | nothing cut off or off the screen |
