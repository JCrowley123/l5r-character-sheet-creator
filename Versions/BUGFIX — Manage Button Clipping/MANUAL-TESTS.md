# Manage Button Clipping — iPhone check

Two minutes, on the branch preview, in portrait and then in landscape.

1. Open any character. The header shows **Characters**, **Save**, **⋯** and **Manage**.
2. Tap **Manage**. The label becomes **Done** cleanly: no stray letter beside it, nothing cut off.
   **Characters**, **Save** and **⋯** stay exactly where they were.
3. Tap **Done**. The label becomes **Manage**; again nothing else in the header moves.
4. Tap it several times quickly, then turn the phone to landscape and repeat.

**Look for:** Done sitting in the middle of a button as wide as Manage's (a little empty space either
side of the word is expected: that is the fix); no letter left behind on any tap.

Report anything that still clips, or a header that jumps.
