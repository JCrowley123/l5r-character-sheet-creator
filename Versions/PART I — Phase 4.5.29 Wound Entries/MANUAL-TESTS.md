# Wound Entries — owner checks

Part of the combined checklist for Phases 4.5.29 and 4.5.30; the published doc is linked from the build
ledger once both are merged and live. Each check is Pass, Fail or Not run; note the device.

Before you start: refresh the live app, use a copy of a character, tap **Manage**. Wounds are on the Combat
tab's wound track (the bar, its +/− buttons and the line under it); tap a segment to see that rank's text.
Earth is the lower of Stamina and Willpower.

## Strength of the Earth (Advantage)

| # | Check | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| W1.1 | Add **Strength of the Earth**; look at its row | "In effect: every Wound Rank's penalty is 3 lower, never below none." |
| W1.2 | Take Wounds until **Nicked** | The track reads no TN penalty, with "(Strength of the Earth −3)" |
| W1.3 | Roll any Trait | No penalty is taken off, and the preview shows a "Wound Penalty" line reading "3 → 0 (Strength of the Earth −3) — … which this cancels" |
| W1.4 | Take Wounds until **Grazed**; roll again | The penalty is 2, not 5, and the line says "5 → 2" |

## Low Pain Threshold (Disadvantage)

| # | Check | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| W2.1 | Remove Strength of the Earth; add **Low Pain Threshold**; Wounds at 0 | Healthy still shows no penalty |
| W2.2 | Take Wounds until **Nicked** | The track reads TN +8 "(Low Pain Threshold +5)"; a roll's Wound Penalty is 8 |
| W2.3 | Add Strength of the Earth back | Nicked reads +5 "(Low Pain Threshold +5, Strength of the Earth −3)" |

## Bad Health and Permanent Wound (Disadvantages)

| # | Check | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| W3.1 | Earth 3; note how many Wounds Healthy holds; add **Bad Health** | Healthy and every later rank now hold what they would at Earth 2; the row says "use Earth 2 (yours is 3)" |
| W3.2 | Set Earth to 1 | The row says Earth 1 is the lowest the ranks can use; the track is the same as without Bad Health |
| W3.3 | Remove Bad Health; add **Permanent Wound**; Wounds at 0 | Still Healthy; the track's maximum is smaller by what Healthy held |
| W3.4 | Take 1 Wound | **Nicked** straight away |
