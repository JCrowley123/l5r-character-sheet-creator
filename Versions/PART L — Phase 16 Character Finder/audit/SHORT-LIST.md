# Short list for the group

Judgement calls the evidence could not settle (rating audit, step 5). For each: keep, change or remove, and why.

- **Hiruma Bushi**: Complexity: rated Straightforward, rubric says Some extra (Rank 1 has: while). Evidence so far: Rank 1 boosts attacks only in the Attack Stance, the ordinary fighting stance, plus a rations perk. Strictly a condition, but nothing to spend or choose.
- **Hiruma Bushi**: Near-twin of "Hiruma Scout [Bushi]" (rated 86% alike): what separates them? Propose a label for each that the evidence supports. Evidence so far: The Bushi School is an evasive melee fighter who picks off key foes, with Stealth only as a Skill. The Scout School (Imperial Histories 1 p.147) is built on Stealth: Initiative from it, near-invisibility and striking from hiding. Proposal: Hiruma Bushi main skirmisher and monster-hunter; Hiruma Scout main stealth. The rubric has no scout label.
- **Kuni Shugenja**: Is the home "library" right? Libraries, temples and places of study. Evidence so far: Their study is of Shadowlands creatures for fighting them; the entry names no library, temple or school as their place. Their setting is the Wall.
- **Toritaka Bushi**: Is "beast" right? Fights or works with an animal companion. Evidence so far: Animal Handling (Falcons) is a School Skill and the Techniques are all named for the falcon, but none involves an animal. A weak also label.
- **Toritaka Bushi**: Is the home "wilds" right? Forests and wild places. Evidence so far: Hunting is taught at rank 2, but the text is about fighting spirit-realm creatures and says nothing of forests or wild country.
- **Yasuki Courtier**: Near-twin of "Yoritomo Courtier" (rated 83% alike): what separates them? Propose a label for each that the evidence supports. Evidence so far: Yasuki win people by finding and supplying what they want (a Commerce bonus, reading desires, a social bonus after providing it). Yoritomo (Core Rulebook p.121) win by bullying and work with pirates and lawless types. Proposal: Yasuki merchant, also diplomat and schemer; Yoritomo schemer and merchant with the sea home. Dropping the Yasuki sea home separates them.
- **Daidoji Iron Warrior [Bushi]**: Is "commander" right? Leads troops: Battle, mass battle, or bonuses to allies in a skirmish. Evidence so far: Battle is a School Skill, but the text makes them the rank and file of the Crane armies, not their leaders, and no Technique helps troops. A weak also label.
- **Daidoji Iron Warrior [Bushi]**: Near-twin of "Lion Elite Spearmen [Bushi]" (rated 100% alike): what separates them? Propose a label for each that the evidence supports. Evidence so far: The Daidoji are bodyguards first: three Techniques build on the Guard Maneuver and taking blows for a charge. The Spearmen (Naishou Province p.7) are spear and polearm specialists who also hurl javelins and have no guarding Technique. Proposal: Daidoji main bodyguard and front line; Spearmen front line without bodyguard.
- **Doji Magistrate [Bushi]**: Is "bodyguard" right? Protects others: yojimbo, guarding a charge, defending allies. Evidence so far: Law officers who subdue and disarm rather than kill; students mostly serve as yoriki or patrol guards. No Technique guards another person, though the family's aim is to protect its people.
- **Doji Magistrate [Bushi]**: Is the home "road" right? The road: wanderers, messengers, travelling monks. Evidence so far: Students patrol borders and keep order in cities and towns; Horsemanship is taught but nothing speaks of wandering. Keeping order in towns fits city streets better than the road.
- **Kakita Bushi**: Is "artist" right? Creates or performs: art, performance, storytelling, beauty. Evidence so far: Tea Ceremony is a School Skill and the family prizes the arts, but the book stresses that the School teaches the duel almost alone. A weak also label.
- **Kakita Bushi**: Should "skirmisher" be added? Fast and mobile: movement, Initiative or hit-and-run Techniques. Evidence so far: Not added. Their Initiative bonus serves to strike first, in the duel and the skirmish; there is no movement or hit-and-run Technique.
- **Kitsuki Investigator [Courtier]**: Is "scholar" right? Knows things: several Lore Skills, study, learning or libraries. Evidence so far: Only one free Lore Skill is taught. The School prizes evidence, keen observation and near-perfect memory, which is detective work rather than study.
- **Mirumoto Bushi**: Near-twin of "Mirumoto Taoist Swordsman [Bushi]" (rated 100% alike): what separates them? Propose a label for each that the evidence supports. Evidence so far: Mirumoto Bushi fight with two swords and have a duelling Technique (Rank 2 adds Kenjutsu to iaijutsu rolls in the Center Stance). The Taoist Swordsman (Emerald Empire p.221) fights unarmoured with one sword, gaining Reduction, and has no duelling Technique. Proposal: Mirumoto keep duelist; Taoist Swordsman lose duelist (its own answer) and gain front line for the Reduction.
- **The Togashi Tattooed Order [Monk]**: Is "traveller" right? Always on the move: wanderers, roads, journeys beyond their Clan's lands. Evidence so far: The Core entry does not describe them as wanderers; only one sample tattoo (Centipede) lets them cross great distances in a day.
- **The Togashi Tattooed Order [Monk]**: Is the home "road" right? The road: wanderers, messengers, travelling monks. Evidence so far: Nothing in the Core entry places them on the road; the support for this home is their reputation as roaming monks, not this page.
- **The Hoshi Tsurui Zumi Order [Monk]**: Near-twin of "Shinmaki Order [Monk]" (rated 75% alike): what separates them? Propose a label for each that the evidence supports. Evidence so far: Both are monks devoted to the spirit, but the Shinmaki (Emerald Empire p.206) are isolated Brotherhood scholars of an obscure sutra with Divination and a gift with commoners; the Tsurui Zumi are Dragon tattooed monks with kiho and unarmed training. Proposal: Shinmaki also spirits (Divination, telling fortunes); Hoshi also unarmed.
- **Ikoma Lion’s Shadow [Bushi]**: Is "stealth" right? Works unseen: Stealth, ambush, disguise, infiltration or scouting. Evidence so far: Stealth is a School Skill and the group is described as small and secretive, but no Technique uses Stealth. Arguably an also label rather than a main one.
- **Kitsu Shugenja**: Is the home "library" right? Libraries, temples and places of study. Evidence so far: Their practice is ancient ritual and contact with ancestors, which suits temples, but the entry names no temple or library; their other focus is magic for the battlefield.
- **Lion Elite Spearmen [Bushi]**: Is "commander" right? Leads troops: Battle, mass battle, or bonuses to allies in a skirmish. Evidence so far: Battle is a School Skill and they serve as units within the Lion armies, but no Technique leads or aids troops. A weak also label.
- **Matsu Beastmaster [Bushi]**: Is the home "wilds" right? Forests and wild places. Evidence so far: They live among half-tamed warcats and are known as wild and uncivilised, serving on raids and reconnaissance; but no forest or wild country is named.
