# Short list for the group

Judgement calls the evidence could not settle (rating audit, step 5). For each: keep, change or remove, and why.

- **Hiruma Bushi**: Complexity: rated Straightforward, rubric says Some extra (Rank 1 has: while). Evidence so far: Rank 1 boosts attacks only in the Attack Stance, the ordinary fighting stance, plus a rations perk. Strictly a condition, but nothing to spend or choose.
- **Hiruma Bushi**: Near-twin of "Hiruma Scout [Bushi]" (rated 86% alike): what separates them? Propose a label for each that the evidence supports. Evidence so far: The Bushi School is an evasive melee fighter who picks off key foes, with Stealth only as a Skill. The Scout School (Imperial Histories 1 p.147) is built on Stealth: Initiative from it, near-invisibility and striking from hiding. Proposal: Hiruma Bushi main skirmisher and monster-hunter; Hiruma Scout main stealth. The rubric has no scout label.
- **Kuni Shugenja**: Is the home "library" right? Libraries, temples and places of study. Evidence so far: Their study is of Shadowlands creatures for fighting them; the entry names no library, temple or school as their place. Their setting is the Wall.
- **Toritaka Bushi**: Is "beast" right? Fights or works with an animal companion. Evidence so far: Animal Handling (Falcons) is a School Skill and the Techniques are all named for the falcon, but none involves an animal. A weak also label.
- **Toritaka Bushi**: Is the home "wilds" right? Forests and wild places. Evidence so far: Hunting is taught at rank 2, but the text is about fighting spirit-realm creatures and says nothing of forests or wild country.
- **Yasuki Courtier**: Near-twin of "Yoritomo Courtier" (rated 83% alike): what separates them? Propose a label for each that the evidence supports. Evidence so far: Yasuki win people by finding and supplying what they want (a Commerce bonus, reading desires, a social bonus after providing it). Yoritomo (Core Rulebook p.121) win by bullying and work with pirates and lawless types. Proposal: Yasuki merchant, also diplomat and schemer; Yoritomo schemer and merchant with the sea home. Dropping the Yasuki sea home separates them.
- **Daidoji Iron Warrior [Bushi]**: Is "commander" right? Leads troops: Battle, mass battle, or bonuses to allies in a skirmish. Evidence so far: Battle is a School Skill, but the text makes them the rank and file of the Crane armies, not their leaders, and no Technique helps troops. A weak also label.
- **Daidoji Iron Warrior [Bushi]**: Near-twin of "Lion Elite Spearmen [Bushi]" (rated 100% alike): what separates them? Propose a label for each that the evidence supports. Evidence so far: The Daidoji are bodyguards first: three Techniques build on the Guard Maneuver and taking blows for a charge. The Spearmen (Naishou Province p.7) are spear and polearm specialists who also hurl javelins and have no guarding Technique. Proposal: Daidoji main bodyguard and front line; Spearmen front line without bodyguard.
- **Doji Magistrate [Bushi]**: Is "bodyguard" right? Protects others: yojimbo, guarding a charge, defending allies. Evidence so far: Law officers who subdue and disarm rather than kill; students mostly serve as yoriki or patrol guards. No Technique guards another person, though the family's aim is to protect its people.
- **Doji Magistrate [Bushi]**: Is the home "road" right? The road: wanderers, messengers, travelling monks. Evidence so far: Students patrol borders and keep order in cities and towns; Horsemanship is taught but nothing speaks of wandering. Keeping order in towns fits city streets better than the road.
- **Kakita Bushi**: Is "artist" right? Creates or performs: art, performance, storytelling, beauty. Evidence so far: Tea Ceremony is a School Skill and the family prizes the arts, but the book stresses that the School teaches the duel almost alone. A weak also label.
- **Kakita Bushi**: Should "skirmisher" be added? Fast and mobile: movement, Initiative or hit-and-run Techniques. Evidence so far: Not added. Their Initiative bonus serves to strike first, in the duel and the skirmish; there is no movement or hit-and-run Technique.
- **Kitsuki Investigator [Courtier]**: Is "scholar" right? Knows things: several Lore Skills, study, learning or libraries. Evidence so far: Only one free Lore Skill is taught. The School prizes evidence, keen observation and near-perfect memory, which is detective work rather than study.
- **Mirumoto Bushi**: Near-twin of "Mirumoto Taoist Swordsman [Bushi]" (rated 100% alike): what separates them? Propose a label for each that the evidence supports. Evidence so far: Mirumoto Bushi fight with two swords and have a duelling Technique (Rank 2 adds Kenjutsu to iaijutsu rolls in the Center Stance). The Taoist Swordsman (Emerald Empire p.221) fights unarmoured with one sword, gaining Reduction, and has no duelling Technique. Proposal: Mirumoto keep duelist; Taoist Swordsman lose duelist (its own answer) and gain front line for the Reduction.
- **The Togashi Tattooed Order [Monk]**: Is "traveller" right? Always on the move: wanderers, roads, journeys beyond their Clan's lands. Evidence so far: The Core entry does not describe them as wanderers; only one sample tattoo (Centipede) lets them cross great distances in a day.
- **The Togashi Tattooed Order [Monk]**: Is the home "road" right? The road: wanderers, messengers, travelling monks. Evidence so far: Nothing in the Core entry places them on the road; the support for this home is their reputation as roaming monks, not this page.
- **The Hoshi Tsurui Zumi Order [Monk]**: Near-twin of "Shinmaki Order [Monk]" (rated 75% alike): what separates them? Propose a label for each that the evidence supports. Evidence so far: Both are monks devoted to the spirit, but the Shinmaki (Emerald Empire p.206) are isolated Brotherhood scholars of an obscure sutra with Divination and a gift with commoners; the Tsurui Zumi are Dragon tattooed monks with kiho and unarmed training. Proposal: Shinmaki also spirits (Divination, telling fortunes); Hoshi also unarmed.
- **Ikoma Lion’s Shadow [Bushi]**: Is "stealth" right? Works unseen: Stealth, ambush, disguise, infiltration or scouting. Evidence so far: Stealth is a School Skill and the group is described as small and secretive, but no Technique uses Stealth. Arguably an also label rather than a main one.
- **Kitsu Shugenja**: Is the home "library" right? Libraries, temples and places of study. Evidence so far: Their practice is ancient ritual and contact with ancestors, which suits temples, but the entry names no temple or library; their other focus is magic for the battlefield.
- **Lion Elite Spearmen [Bushi]**: Is "commander" right? Leads troops: Battle, mass battle, or bonuses to allies in a skirmish. Evidence so far: Battle is a School Skill and they serve as units within the Lion armies, but no Technique leads or aids troops. A weak also label.
- **Matsu Beastmaster [Bushi]**: Is the home "wilds" right? Forests and wild places. Evidence so far: They live among half-tamed warcats and are known as wild and uncivilised, serving on raids and reconnaissance; but no forest or wild country is named.
- **Agasha Shugenja**: Is "battle-magic" right? A spellcaster whose magic destroys enemies. Evidence so far: The entry is about experiment, alchemy and blending elements, and the Technique lets them swap the Ring used to cast; nothing speaks of destroying foes beyond their Fire affinity.
- **Asako Henshin [Monk]**: Should "unarmed" be added? Fights without weapons: grappling, wrestling or martial arts. Evidence so far: Jiujutsu is a School Skill and Rank 4 raises their unarmed damage, but the School centres on bending Traits and riddling the elements, so at most an also label.
- **Asako Henshin [Monk]**: Complexity: rated Involved, rubric says Some extra (Rank 1 has: Contested, Raises?, if, per, unaware). Evidence so far: No spells, kiho or tattoos appear in the entry, so the rubric makes it Some extra; but its Rank 1 power, raising or lowering Traits a set number of times a day, is a lot for a new player to track.
- **Asako Henshin [Monk]**: Near-twin of "First Dawn Scholars [Monk]" (rated 100% alike): what separates them? Propose a label for each that the evidence supports. Evidence so far: The Henshin are a secretive Phoenix sect seeking perfection of the soul by tricking the elements. The First Dawn Scholars (Imperial Histories 2 p.29) are the Owl Clan's order in the Togashi Dynasty, keeping a great library on the ancient pre-human world. Proposal: First Dawn main scholar with the library home and Unusual; Henshin main mystic, also unarmed.
- **Isawa Shugenja**: Is "battle-magic" right? A spellcaster whose magic destroys enemies. Evidence so far: The book says the School has no particular specialisation and is simply the finest at casting in general, with a chosen Affinity; destruction is not its theme, though nothing stops an Isawa from being a battle mage.
- **Shiba Bushi**: Is "diplomat" right? Wins people over: courtesy, alliances, favours, mediation and calm. Evidence so far: Described as peaceful warrior-scholars who seek any option short of killing, but they learn no social Skill and no Technique persuades anyone. A weak also label.
- **Shiba Bushi**: Is the home "court" right? Courts and castles: a Courtier School, or the description centres on court life. Evidence so far: Their role is yojimbo to the Isawa and warrior-scholar; the entry never mentions court life.
- **Bayushi Bushi**: Is the home "underworld" right? City streets, the underworld, criminals and shadows. Evidence so far: Taught manipulation, stealth and sometimes poison as weapons of discord; no city streets or criminals are named.
- **Shosuro Infiltrator [Ninja]**: Is "skirmisher" right? Fast and mobile: movement, Initiative or hit-and-run Techniques. Evidence so far: Their speed comes only from striking out of hiding (Rank 3); there is no movement, Initiative or hit-and-run Technique. Ambush belongs under stealth.
- **Soshi Magistrate [Bushi]**: Complexity: rated Some extra, rubric says Straightforward (Rank 1 is a constant bonus). Evidence so far: Rank 1 is mostly constant (an Intimidation bonus and melee damage) but the bonus grows against lower Status, a small condition.
- **Soshi Shugenja**: Is "strange-magic" right? Unusual magic: alchemy, experiment, talismans, foreign or multi-element spells. Evidence so far: Silent casting and illusions are not among the rubric's kinds of unusual magic (alchemy, experiment, talismans, foreign or multi-element).
- **Yogo Wardmaster [Shugenja]**: Is "monster-hunter" right? Hunts Shadowlands creatures, the Tainted, maho-tsukai or otherworldly beings. Evidence so far: The School is about paper wards and protective charms. Taint-hunting belongs to the Kuroiban Alternate Path on the next page, not to the School itself.
- **Horiuchi Shugenja**: Near-twin of "Iuchi Shugenja" (rated 80% alike): what separates them? Propose a label for each that the evidence supports. Evidence so far: Horiuchi make meishodo amulets, a blend of gaijin and Rokugani magic that stores one spell for quick casting, and the Core Rulebook's family entry (p.129) says they specialise in defensive and protective magic. The Iuchi (Core Rulebook p.130) speed others' movement and favour Travel spells. Proposal: Horiuchi main unusual magic, also protective magic in place of traveller; Iuchi main traveller and unusual magic.
- **Iuchi Shugenja**: Is "nature" right? Tied to nature: animals, forests, weather, the sea and storms. Evidence so far: The entry speaks of open-plains teaching, talismans and Travel magic; nothing about weather, animals, forests or the sea.
- **Iuchi Shugenja**: Should "skirmisher" be added? Fast and mobile: movement, Initiative or hit-and-run Techniques. Evidence so far: Their Technique gives an ally an extra Move Action and they favour Travel spells, which meets the rubric's movement test, though the label fits a caster awkwardly.
- **Moto Vindicator [Bushi]**: Is the home "road" right? The road: wanderers, messengers, travelling monks. Evidence so far: Sworn to destroy the Tainted Moto and trained with the Crab; the entry is about the Shadowlands, not wandering.
- **Utaku Battle Maiden [Bushi]**: Should "skirmisher" be added? Fast and mobile: movement, Initiative or hit-and-run Techniques. Evidence so far: Not added. Rank 2 can add to Initiative and Rank 4 charges, but both serve mounted combat, which the cavalry label already covers.
- **Utaku Mounted Infantry [Bushi]**: Complexity: rated Straightforward, rubric says Some extra (Rank 1 has: choose). Evidence so far: Rank 1's only choice is a weapon picked once when the character is made; after that it is a steady bonus. Arguably Some extra by the rubric's letter.
- **The Order of the Spider Monks [Monk]**: Is the home "road" right? The road: wanderers, messengers, travelling monks. Evidence so far: The entry mentions spreading their founder's teaching but never wandering or travel.
- **The Order of the Spider Monks [Monk]**: Complexity: rated Involved, rubric says Some extra (Rank 1 has: against). Evidence so far: None of the five Techniques is a kiho, spell or tattoo, so the rubric gives Some extra; whether these monks may also buy kiho under the general monk rules is for the group.
- **Seppun Guardsman [Bushi]**: Is "frontline" right? Built to stand in melee and take hits: heavy weapons or armour, Reduction, endurance. Evidence so far: They wear light armour and their defence is about the charge: spotting threats and, at Rank 5, taking a blow meant for the one they guard. Nothing gives Reduction or endurance.
- **Seppun Guardsman [Bushi]**: Should "investigator" be added? Finds the truth: Investigation, evidence, interrogation, magistrates and hunting fugitives. Evidence so far: Rank 1 aids Investigation against ambushes and Rank 3 sees through every disguise and illusion, but this serves the guard's watchfulness, not detective work.
- **Moshi Shugenja**: Near-twin of "Yoritomo Shugenja" (rated 100% alike): what separates them? Propose a label for each that the evidence supports. Evidence so far: The Moshi are devoted to the Sun, gain a Fire Affinity by day, favour Thunder spells and call the weather kami, posted on Mantis ships to calm the seas. Yoritomo shugenja (The Great Clans p.168) are bound to the sea itself, change wind and weather by Technique and learn Sailing. Proposal: Yoritomo Shugenja main nature and sailor; Moshi main nature, also mystic for their Sun devotion in place of sailor.
