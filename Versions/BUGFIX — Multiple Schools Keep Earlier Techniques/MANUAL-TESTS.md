# Multiple Schools Keep Earlier Techniques — iPhone check

About five minutes, on the branch preview. Use a throwaway copy (Characters list, ⋯ on any
character, **Save As a copy**) so nothing you care about changes.

1. Open the copy and tap **Manage**. On **Clan & School**, apply **Crab → Hida Bushi**.
2. On **Identity**, raise **Other Insight Bonus** until **Insight Rank** reads **3**. The
   **Techniques** tab lists Hida's three: The Way of the Crab, The Mountain Does Not Move, Two
   Pincers One Mind.
3. On **Advantages**, add **Multiple Schools**. On **Skills**, add **Hunting**, **Kyujutsu** and
   **Stealth** at Rank 1 (Hiruma Bushi's other School Skills; Hida already gave the rest).
4. On **Identity**, tap **+ Add School** and pick **Hiruma Bushi**. The Schools panel shows Hida
   locked at Rank 3 and Hiruma at Rank 0.
   **Check: Techniques still lists Hida's three.** Before the fix they vanished here.
5. Raise **Other Insight Bonus** by 25. Techniques now lists Hida's three **and** Torch's Flame
   Flickers (Hiruma Rank 1).
6. Tap **Save**, go back to **Characters**, open the copy again: all four are still there. Tap
   **Done**: Play lists the same four.
7. Delete the copy when you have finished.

**Look for:** the first School's Techniques staying put in steps 4 to 6, and no Technique listed
twice. Report anything missing, doubled, or tagged with the wrong School (each Technique's
description begins with its School and Rank).
