Mass Battle and Mass Combat are accepted names for the Battle Emphasis: the source entries use both terms. Merely typing a Level 4 spell on the sheet does not prove casting eligibility. Casting applies School Rank, affinity and deficiency, includes qualifying frozen Shugenja training after subtracting later Alternate Path ranks, and permits Void only with Ishiken-Do. The active-School calculation reuses the existing casting-rank function.

The sheet's Minor Clan library includes historical Mantis. Its Clan field does not distinguish the eras; the campaign must actually treat Mantis as a Minor Clan for Minor Clan Defender. The source does not impose a universal Clan gate on every clan-labelled Advanced School.

## Progression and automation boundary

All supported entries reuse the Core release's three-rank progression, owner-required Multiple Schools gate, frozen Basic School rank, and save/load record. They grant no starting Traits, Skills, Honor, outfit, or free first rank. Technique effects are reference text, including Kiho, Servants, casting-rank bonuses and spell slots. Later automatic effects belong to the Synergy Engine; this release does not silently change combat calculations.

Berserkers is one of the fourteen indexed entries but is not a fourteenth playable human School. The completed catalogue contains 23 references: 22 can be used by the current human-character model, subject to their requirements. Nonhuman support is a separate remaining boundary.

The existing Alternate Path system addresses Basic School ranks. Akodo Tactical Master's Advanced-rank replacement clause remains a recorded, unavailable clause; adding Advanced-rank Path selection would be an extension to that progression system, not a Technique effect implied by this catalogue release.

Scorpion Instigator's four separate Blackmail purchases remain unchanged and are explicitly deferred by the owner to Phase 15 or later.
