# Damage, Sessions and XP — owner checks

Part of the combined checklist for Phases 4.5.31 and 4.5.32; the published doc is linked from the build
ledger once both are merged and live. Each check is Pass, Fail or Not run; note the device.

Before you start: refresh the live app, use a copy of a character, tap **Manage**, and add the entry named
in each row from the Advantages (or Disadvantages) picker.

| # | Add | Do | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Hands of Stone** | look at an Unarmed weapon row's damage; roll its Damage | one more kept die (e.g. 3k1 becomes 3k2); the result names "Hands of Stone" |
| D2 | **Large** | a No-dachi (or other Large melee weapon) and a Katana | +1k0 on the No-dachi's damage only |
| D3 | **Small** | a Katana and a bow | −1k0 on the Katana's damage; the bow unchanged |
| D4 | Large, then open the Disadvantages picker | find Small | greyed, "— not with Large" (and Large greyed beside Small) |
| D5 | **Great Destiny** (or **Dark Fate**) | tap **Use**, then OK | Wounds set to one less than the most you can take (the track shows Out); the row says Used this session and Use is greyed |
| D6 | the same | tap **Reset session** | Available this session again |
| D7 | **Haunted** | turn on **Ancestor angry**; roll anything, tick "Haunted: the GM chose this roll", roll | −1k1 on that roll; the row then says Used this session and the box is no longer offered |
| D8 | the same | **Reset session** | the box is offered again |
| D9 | **Enlightened** | raise Void by one Rank | the Void XP is 2 less than without it |
| D10 | **Obtuse** | look at Courtier (a High Skill) and Investigation at the same Rank | Courtier's Rank XP doubled; Investigation unchanged; an Emphasis still 2 XP |
| D11 | **Blissful Betrothal**, then **Social Position** | look at Social Position's cost and the XP spent | 4 instead of 6, and XP spent agrees at once |
| D12 | the same, plus a **Kharmic Tie** | turn on "Your Kharmic Tie is to your spouse" | the Tie costs 2 less (never below 1) |
| D13 | Haunted angry, a Destiny used, the Betrothal discount; save and reload | look at the rows and costs | all as you left them; Social Position still 4 |
| D14 | Any of them, then tap **Done** | the Use, Reset and Ancestor angry controls | still work in Play mode; the spouse switch is locked |
| D15 | iPhone, portrait | the Haunted, Great Destiny and Blissful Betrothal rows | nothing cut off or off the screen |
