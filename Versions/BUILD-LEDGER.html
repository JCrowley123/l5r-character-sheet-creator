Also shipped, outside the phase numbering

The removability contract. Phases 1, 2 and 9 were hardened so any one can be surgically removed without touching the others — guarded hooks, per-phase comment markers, and a feature-dependencies.py check that attributes every reference to whichever phase owns it. Proven by removing each in a scratch copy: the other two passed in full every time. It's now the standing rule in CLAUDE.md for all future features, including how to declare a genuine dependency.
