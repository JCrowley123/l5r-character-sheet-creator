A19 · FT-24 · your ruling of 9 October · Q7

Undo the most recent Void card spend

The Void card is on the Combat page. Your ruling on 9 October: an Undo on the Void card for the most recent spend made there, which gives the point back, takes back its effect and drops its Willpower roll, until the next roll, Next Round or Reset. The audit adds: put back the exact values, once, and keep it apart from the roll preview's refund and from History.

The rule

WhatProposed
CoversThe spends made from the Void card: its six buttons (+1k1; Unskilled roll made Skilled; +10 TN; +10 Initiative; damage reduction; Kiho) and Hotei's Curse's two-point button when that Curse is on the sheet (Undo gives both points back).
Puts backExactly the values from just before that spend, once: the Void Points; the armed one-roll effect (including one it replaced: Skilled, then +1k1, then Undo leaves Skilled armed); the Round's Void spend; Armor TN; skirmish Initiative; the Wounds a damage reduction added.
Touch of the VoidA Willpower roll still waiting for that spend is dropped. One you cancelled is not owed again. One you rolled is a roll, so Undo has already gone.
Goes awayAt any roll the sheet makes (a preview you cancel is not a roll); Next Round; Reset; another card spend (Undo then covers only that one); any other change to the Void Points, the armed effect, the Round or the Wounds (a pip tap, a Void Point spent in a roll preview, loading another character).
OnceAfter Undo the line reads "Undone: …" with no button; a double tap undoes once.
Not coveredVoid Points spent outside the card: the roll preview's own Void option (its roll follows at once), Lost Love's button, Dark Paragon's Determination, an Ancestor's gift, a pip tap. The preview's refund when a roll is cancelled stays as it is, apart from this. History is unchanged: it records the spend and the Undo as ordinary Void Points changes, and has no undo of its own.
SavedNothing new. Undo's own state is forgotten when the page closes, like the Round tracker.

A20 · FT-28 · your ruling of 10 October · Q7

Choose a Trait for one Skill roll

The books treat a Skill roll as a Skill and Trait pair that the GM names; each Skill has a usual Trait (Core Rulebook p.79). Some uses name another Trait: the Skill Traits audit of 10 October found eight (for example Athletics with Agility for throwing, p.139; Intimidation with Willpower for Control, pp.121 and 229), and the Book of Earth (pp.8–9) lists Willpower and Stamina pairings a GM may call for, including Willpower for Intimidation where it suits better than Awareness. I re-read those pages for this board.

Laptop · the same roll

The rule

WhatProposed
WhereThe preview of every roll made from a Skill: trained, and Unskilled from the untrained list. Not weapon attacks, Trait or Ring rolls, spells or the dice tray.
Starts onThe Skill's own Trait as the sheet holds it (the catalogue Trait, or one you set by hand in Manage).
ListedThe usual Trait; then any Trait the books name for that Skill, with its page (from the Skill Traits audit and the Book of Earth list); then every other Trait. Void appears only for Iaijutsu (Focus, Core p.87).
ChangesThe dice: (Trait + Skill Rank) keep Trait, or Trait keep Trait when Unskilled (10s still do not explode), and anything the sheet adds that depends on the Trait. The Skill Rank, the Emphasis reroll, the Unskilled rule, Void offers, declarations and every other modifier stay; anything already ticked stays ticked when the Trait changes.
MarkedA Trait that is not the usual one is outlined, with "This roll only; Intimidation is usually Awareness." and, for a book-named Trait, its page.
ResetsEach time the preview opens, it starts on the usual Trait. Cancel forgets the choice.
SavedNothing: the Skill's Trait in the character and the save file does not change, and no XP is worked out from the choice.
About the scratch version: to keep every number the sheet's own, it reopens the preview with the chosen Trait through the sheet's Skill roll. The build will redraw the preview in place instead, so ticks you have already made are kept.
Recommendation: approve A20 as shown, with the book-named Traits listed first. It follows your "remind, not remember" principle: when the GM asks for an unusual pairing, the sheet already shows the one the books name and where. The smaller alternative is a plain list of every Trait with no book pairings; it needs no per-Skill table, but the player has to know which pairing applies.
Your decisions

Three short questions

  1. Grouping. Recommended: one G6 with both units, as planned. They touch different parts of the sheet (the Void card and the roll preview) and are separately removable, and one qualification serves both. The alternative is two groups of one, each qualified and taken live on its own: A19 could go live sooner, at the cost of a second qualification.
  2. A19, Undo the last Void spend: approve as shown (recommended), revise, or drop.
  3. A20, a Trait for one Skill roll: approve with the book-named Traits first (recommended), approve with a plain list, revise, or drop.

Approval permits the build and preview stage only: each unit on its own stacked branch, light checks, one code review, then your device round with Accept / Revise / Remove for each. Qualification and go-live come after, only on your word.
