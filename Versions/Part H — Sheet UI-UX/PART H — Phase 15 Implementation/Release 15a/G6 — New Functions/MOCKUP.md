## The units as approved

| Unit | Approved scope | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| **A19** Undo the last Void spend (FT-24, Q7) | After a spend made from the Void card (Combat page), a line under the card's buttons shows an Undo button and what was spent, for example "Spent: +1k1 on your next roll. Touch of the Void's Willpower roll comes after that roll; Undo drops it." It covers the card's six buttons (+1k1; Unskilled roll made Skilled; +10 TN; +10 Initiative; damage reduction; Kiho) and Hotei's Curse's two-point card button (both points back). Undo puts back exactly the values from just before that spend, once: the Void Points; the armed one-roll effect, including one it replaced (Skilled, then +1k1, then Undo leaves Skilled armed); the Round's Void spend; Armor TN; skirmish Initiative; the Wounds a damage reduction added. A Touch of the Void Willpower roll still waiting for that spend is dropped; one the player cancelled is not owed again; one rolled is a roll. Undo goes away at any roll the sheet makes (a cancelled preview is not a roll), Next Round, Reset, another card spend (Undo then covers only that one), and any other change to the Void Points, the armed effect, the Round or the Wounds (a pip tap, a Void Point spent in a roll preview, loading another character). After Undo the line reads "Undone: …" with no button; a double tap undoes once. Not covered: Void Points spent outside the card (the roll preview's own Void option, Lost Love, Dark Paragon's Determination, an Ancestor's gift, pip taps). The after-preview refund (RF7) and History (AL71) are unchanged and separate; History records the spend and the Undo as ordinary Void Points changes. Nothing new is saved. | Behaviour |
| **A20** A Trait for one Skill roll (FT-28, Q7) | The Trait line of the preview of every roll made from a Skill (trained, and Unskilled from the untrained list; not weapon attacks, Trait or Ring rolls, spells or the dice tray) becomes a drop-down showing each Trait with its value. It starts on the Skill's own Trait as the sheet holds it. **Version approved: book Traits first.** The list holds the usual Trait; then any Trait the books name for that Skill, with its page; then every other Trait (the GM's call, Core p.79). Void appears only for Iaijutsu (Focus, Core p.87). A choice changes the dice ((Trait + Skill Rank) keep Trait; Unskilled: Trait keep Trait, 10s still do not explode) and anything the sheet adds that depends on the Trait; the Skill Rank, the Emphasis re-roll, the Unskilled rule, Void offers, declarations and every other modifier stay, and anything already ticked stays ticked (the preview redraws in place). A Trait that is not the usual one is outlined, with "This roll only; Intimidation is usually Awareness." and, for a book-named Trait, its page. Each opening starts on the usual Trait; Cancel forgets the choice. Nothing is saved: the Skill's Trait in the character and the save file does not change, and no XP is worked out. | Behaviour |

Keeping ticks when the Trait changes needs a guarded hook in that render, as Features 4.5.10, 4.5.11 and 4.5.15 added theirs; the scratch version reopened the preview instead. Declare the hook and its removal before building.
- **A19's hooks.** The Void card (Part C Feature 4, `160-feat-void.js`), Touch of the Void's owed count (`VI4533.owed`, `209.99999993-feat-void-initiative-entries.js`), Hotei's two-point spend (`HOTEI4536`, `209.9999999999-feat-weakness-hotei.js`), the damage row, Next Round and Reset (`110-modals-trackers.js`). - Noticed in passing, not in G6's scope: today a pip tap does not redraw the Void card's note ("2 Void Points remaining" with 3 back) until the next redraw. A19's Undo redraws the card itself.
