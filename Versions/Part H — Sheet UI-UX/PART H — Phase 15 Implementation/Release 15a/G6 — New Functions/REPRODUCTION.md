## Book pages re-read for A20

Extracted with `pdftotext` to a temporary folder in the session's scratch space, searched, and deleted before this record was written (the 30 September ruling): Core Rulebook pp.79–81, 87–88, 111, 114, 121, 131, 135–145, 157, 229 and Book of Earth pp.7–10, 51, 202. In our own words: the GM names a Skill/Trait pair for a Skill roll, and each Skill is usually paired with one Trait (Core p.79); the Book of Earth suggests Willpower or Stamina pairings a GM may call for, including Willpower for Intimidation where it suits better than Awareness (pp.8–9); Intimidation (Control) with Willpower appears in School Techniques, against Etiquette (Courtesy) with Willpower (Core pp.121, 229); the Intimidation entry itself (p.144) gives Awareness.
