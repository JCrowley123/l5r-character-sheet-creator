# Qualification attempt 1 failed at the 4× CPU screen: a timing fault in A02's own harness

**What failed.** The first qualification chain (`qualification-1cc41d8c.json`, `under-load-final-1cc41d8c.log`, `under-load-1cc41d8c/`) passed removal (10/10), all 9 negative variants and the dependency check, then ran the 68 affected harnesses at 4× CPU. The first of them, A02's own `floating-strip-harness.js`, scored 22/23: `QA-PANEL-BESIDE-BUTTON` failed with the panel open, below the header and above the band, but `beside: false`. The chain stops on a failure, so the screen ended after 37 harnesses (36 passed) and the discovery run never started. Nothing was re-run to get a pass.

**Cause, measured** (`panel-under-load.cjs`, results `panel-cpu1.json` and `panel-cpu4.json`, three fresh pages each at 1× and 4×). Right after the tap, the Quick Access button is still in its existing 0.1 s `:hover`/`:active` scale transition (Phase 2 (Part H)). Its painted box (`getBoundingClientRect`) is anywhere from left 10.68 px to 13.22 px depending on when it is read. Its layout position (`offsetLeft`) is always 12 px, and the panel's left edge is always 12 px. The check compared the panel with the painted box using a 1 px tolerance, so it passed or failed by when the frame landed. At 1× it happened to read 12.76 px; at 4× it read 13.22 px or 10.68 px. The app is not at fault: the panel is placed exactly where it should be.

**Fix (harness only, the application is unchanged at `1cc41d8c…5d2c`).** The check now compares the panel with the button's layout box (`offsetLeft`/`offsetTop`), which ignores the transform. The 1 px tolerance is unchanged; no check was dropped and no timeout was lengthened. The fixed harness passed 23/23 three times at 4× CPU. Attempt 3 of the chain (`qualification-1cc41d8c-3.json`) then re-runs every stage from removal, including the variants: `qa-unplaced` and `panel-unplaced` must still fail `QA-PANEL-BESIDE-BUTTON`, which shows the fixed check can fail.

**Attempt 2, stopped by hand.** Attempt 2 (`qualification-1cc41d8c-2.json`, `qualify-launch-2.log`, `removal-final-1cc41d8c-2.log`) was launched at 21:30 UTC while attempt 1, unknown to me, was still running the rest of its 4× screen (the screen runs every harness before reporting). It was stopped within its first stage, the removal proof on scratch copies, before any browser stage, so the two never competed for the browser. Attempt 1 ran on to the end of its screen; attempt 3 starts only after it has finished.

**Attempt 1 ran its screen to the end:** 67 of 68 harnesses passed at 4× CPU (3,914 of 3,915 checks); the one failure is the check above.

Pass 2 stopped there: its retained suite and removal did not run. **The owner's rule is not met (both passes must succeed on their first executions), and pass 2 was not re-run.**

**What the record shows.** The leaf took 1,276 s. The same leaf took 86–89 s in each of five earlier runs: A01's discovery and both A01 passes, and A02's discovery and pass 1. `sw-checks.js` gives each harness 1,200 s (`spawnSync` timeout), after which it is killed with no exit code and no error text: that is the 0/0. So `pwa-harness.js` stalled for 20 minutes right after `index.html is cached`. In that stretch it waits, with no limit of its own, on `page.evaluate` (writing the probe character), `server.close` (which waits for open connections) and further `page.evaluate` calls; only the offline reload has a limit (30 s). Windows logged no sleep, wake or power event between 00:05 and 00:40 UTC.

**Reproduction attempt** (`pwa-pass2/`).
