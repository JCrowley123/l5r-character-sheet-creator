## A08 checks

1. Quick burst: three quick taps on Honour's +; "Undo takes back all 3 taps."; Undo returns to the start with "Undone. Honour: Rank …, Points …".
2. Three taps about two seconds apart, Undo straight away: back to the start.
3. One tap: the message as on the live app; Undo takes back that tap.
4. A pause over five seconds between two taps: Undo takes back only the second.
5. Honour + twice, Glory + once, Undo: only Glory goes back.
6. Three taps two seconds apart, wait six seconds: History has one new Honour entry.
7. iPhone: a quick double tap on + counts as two taps (no zoom); Undo takes both back.
8. Two taps then Save: saved, Undo gone; reopened, History has one entry for the two taps.
9. Taint, Glory or Status: three quick taps, Undo back to the start.

## A12 checks

1. A Willpower roll: one closed "Resisting?" line with its count.
2. Tap it open: Honour's choice and its note; tap again to close.
3. Tick Honour's choice inside: the line stays open; the modifiers show the bonus.
4. Closed again: "1 chosen" and "Honour +N"; the roll's total includes it.
5. A character with a resistance Advantage: the count, and opened, Honour first, then the Advantage(s) with notes.
6. The Roll button on screen without scrolling the preview.
7. Choices that are not resistances stay outside the line.
8. A new roll starts with the line closed and nothing ticked.
9. Laptop: Tab to the line, Enter opens, Space closes.
10. Optional: a Blessing, Spirit Realm or resisting Ancestor choice sits inside the line.
