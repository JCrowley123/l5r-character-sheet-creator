A08 · FT-47 · your rulings of 28 October and Q4

One Undo for a burst of quick − / + taps

Honour, Glory, Status and Taint each have − and + buttons, in Play and in Manage. Today each tap is its own change: Undo holds only the last tap and then disappears. You asked for taps close together to count as one change, with a gap of 4–5 seconds because an accidental zoom can cost a few seconds; Q4 settled it as a rolling five-second group.

The rule

WhatProposed
Joins the groupAnother − or + on the same card within 5 seconds of the previous tap. − and + both count, whatever number is in the box between them.
Ends the group5 seconds without a tap; a tap on another card; typing a Rank or Points value; Set Rank; Recognition by your lord; Undo; opening, loading, saving or resetting the character; any other change to the character, such as a roll, a Void Point or an edit elsewhere. Moving between pages or scrolling does not end it.
UndoGoes back to the value before the group's first tap, in one step. It stays on screen exactly as long as it does today (20 seconds after the last tap, or until you save). There is still one Undo at a time: a new change replaces it, as today.
HistoryOne entry for the group, written when the group ends. A group you undo before it ends leaves no entry, as quick taps undone today do. The Undo itself is an entry, as today.
MessageOne tap: word for word as today. Two or more: "Honour: Rank 5, Points 0. Undo takes back all 3 taps." After Undo: "Undone. Honour: Rank 4, Points 7."
UnchangedThe values each tap gives, Rank changes at 10 points, limits, Taint, Recognition, the 20-second Undo window, autosave and the save format. Nothing new is saved.
Recommendation: approve A08 as shown. It is Q4 as ruled. The extra words in the message appear only when a group holds two or more taps, so a single tap reads exactly as today and you can see what Undo will do before you press it.
A12 · R6 · your ruling of 7 October: one closed Resisting? line

The resistance choices in one closed line

The roll preview's "Declare for this roll" lists every choice that might apply to the roll. The resistance choices are offered on every Skill, Trait, Ring and dice-tray roll, because the sheet cannot tell which roll is a resistance. Each takes a line or two for its label and another one or two for its note, and they appear in two or three places: Honour's own choice at the end, Balance, Clear Thinker, Heartless and Irreproachable under Advantages at the start.

Which choices go in the line

Every choice that is a resistance, or a Contested Roll against an effect used on you: fifteen in the sheet today. A character sees only the ones it has.

SourceChoices
HonourResisting Intimidation, Temptation or Fear (Core Rulebook p.91)
AdvantagesBalance, Clear Thinker, Heartless, Irreproachable
Seven Fortunes' BlessingJurojin (poison or disease), Hotei (an effect that would take your Void Points)
Touch of the Spirit RealmsMeido (social manipulation), Tengoku (Shadowlands Taint, Earth Ring rolls only)
AncestorKaimetsu-Uo (provocation), Moto (restraint or mental influence), Togashi Yamatsu (possession or magical control), Matsu Hitomi (Temptation, Intimidation or Fear), Usagi Reichin (Fear, and a Bloodspeaker's Fear)
  • Stays where it is: every other choice, for example Missing Limb, Dangerous Beauty, Imperial Spouse, Precise Memory, Wary and the Ancestors' other gifts, and the separate Void Point, Kharmic Tie and curse boxes above.
  • Unchanged: every bonus and total, Balance including Honour once, Usagi Reichin's "in place of" pair, the choices being cleared for every roll, and nothing being saved. The line changes where the choices are shown, not what they do.
  • The line is a native disclosure, as the Combat sections use, so it works by tap, click and keyboard, and a screen reader announces it as collapsed or expanded.
Recommendation: approve A12 as shown. It is your 7 October ruling and the audit's proposal: closed on every roll, the count on the line, the existing choices inside, and a ticked choice always named.
