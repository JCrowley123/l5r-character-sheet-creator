The × in its own corner on every card

Today the × sits on top of the right end of the XP or Points box on every Technique, Advantage and Disadvantage card (34 × 36 px of overlap on a phone). Proposed: the same corner the spell cards now use. The number box moves left to make room, the × gets a 44 px target, and no card gets taller. In Play, where there is no ×, nothing changes.
