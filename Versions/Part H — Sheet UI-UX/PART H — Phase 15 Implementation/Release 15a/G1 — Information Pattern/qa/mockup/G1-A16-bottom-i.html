A16 · The bottom i in the seven pickers

The 24 September device pass (S1, S2) named seven pickers: A01, A03, A04, A09, A12, A13 and A14, all confirmed in today's build. Each one adds its own i at the foot of the picker. Measured on today's build: in all seven the picker's own note already states the core rule, and after Confirm the row's i opens every sentence the bottom i holds (checked for all seven: nothing is lost). While you are choosing, the bottom i adds only detail, listed below, chiefly the price in A03, A09 and A12.
