Clan, Family & School

Pick your Clan, Family, and School, then apply each — it fills in trait bonuses, starting Honor, starting Skills, and starting Outfit for you. Technique names are listed for reference only; check your rulebook (or sourcebook noted) for the exact rules text.

Identity

Insight = (sum of all five Rings × 10) + total Skill Ranks + Insight Bonus. Insight Rank breakpoints: 0–149 Rank 1, 150–174 Rank 2, 175–199 Rank 3, then +25 per Rank. School Rank tracks Insight Rank automatically (and never decreases if Insight drops) — School Techniques for your current School unlock into the Techniques list below as you reach each Rank.

Rings & Traits

Ring rank normally equals the lower of its two traits — adjust freely if kata, advantages, or void points change this.

Skills

Roll = Skill Rank + Trait Rank, Keep = Trait Rank (adjust manually for High/Low or trait-only rolls). Pick a skill from the dropdown to add it with its Trait and Mastery notes already filled in, or use "+ Add Skill" for a custom one. Emphases: 1 at Rank 1, 2 at Rank 3, 3 at Rank 5, 4 at Rank 7, 5 at Rank 9 — 2 xp each, added from each skill's relevant list or as a custom entry.

Advantages & Disadvantages

Pick from the reference library to add a pre-filled entry (name, base point cost, and a short paraphrase of the effect) — costs shown are the base/example cost, so adjust the Points field for variable-cost, per-rank, or clan-discounted versions, and check your rulebook for the exact rules text. Use "+ Add" for a custom entry instead.

Techniques, Kata & Spells

Kiho: spend a Void Point (Free Action), or roll Meditation / Void vs. TN 15 (Complex) or TN 30 (Simple).

Spell Slots

Maximum slots per element always equal that element's Ring Rank. Click a filled pip to spend it; click (or right-click) a blank pip to get it back.

Combat

Base TN = (Reflexes × 5) + 5. Current TN adds your Armor TN Bonus. Initiative = (Reflexes + Insight Rank)k(Reflexes).
Attack = Weapon Skill Rank + Trait, keep Trait. Damage = weapon DR + Strength (Perception for gunpowder weapons; a bow's own Strength rating for bows), plus any Mastery bonus your Skill Rank has unlocked. Pick a weapon from the dropdown to fill its DR, Size and Skill automatically; typing over the Attack or Damage box switches that row to manual and rolls exactly what you typed.
