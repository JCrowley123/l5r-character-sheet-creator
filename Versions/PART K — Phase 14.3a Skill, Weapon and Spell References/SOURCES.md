# Verified entry pages — Skills, Weapons and Spells (Phase 14.3a)

Located on 14 October 2026 in the owner's 16 sourcebook PDFs. Text was extracted to a scratch folder outside the
repository (xpdf `pdftotext`, reading order); nothing extracted is stored here. Printed page = PDF page − 3 in the
Core Rulebook, − 1 in every other book used here (`SOURCEBOOK INDEX — Page Map/index.json`).

## How each entry start was found

- **Spells:** a line naming the spell followed within a few lines by its `Ring/Mastery:` line (Core, Enemies of the
  Empire, Secrets of the Empire, The Great Clans), or Book of Air's new-spell layout (`[Keyword]` brackets). Core's
  two-column text sometimes joins a heading to the end of the previous line; those were accepted only in capitals.
- **Skills:** the `NAME (TRAIT)` heading followed by its `Sub-types:` line.
- **Weapons:** the weapon's capitalised heading in Core's Equipment chapter (pp.199–205); arrows are the arrow-type
  list on p.199; Unarmed is the unarmed-damage rule in Core's Damage Rolls section (p.80). Gunpowder weapons and
  skills are Imperial Histories 1's "new weapon" and "new Bugei skill" entries.
- **Rendered and looked at:** Core pp.80 and 205 (PDF 83 and 208), where the extracted text did not show the heading.

## Entries printed in more than one book

**None.** No Skill, Weapon or Spell in the sheet is printed in full in two books, so rules 3 and 4 (a later book
changing an entry) do not arise in 14.3a. Several later books **discuss** entries without reprinting their rules;
these are not printings and are not cited:

- Spells: Book of Air pp.78–89 (45 spells across both books carry a `NAME (RING N)` commentary heading), Book of Void
  pp.61–67, Book of Earth pp.71–76, Book of Fire pp.54–59 and Book of Water pp.59–60.
- Weapons: Book of Water pp.20–33 ("The Martial Paths of Water") and Book of Void pp.26–27. The Scimitar in
  Enemies of the Empire p.86 is the Naga weapon, a different item.

## Mastery and keywords were not compared

A comparison of each printing's Ring/Mastery line with the catalogue was tried and abandoned: Core's two-column
text interleaves neighbouring spells, so the line after a heading is often the next spell's (for example, Call Upon
the Wind is followed by two Ring/Mastery lines, Air 1 and Air 2). No catalogue discrepancy is reported from it. The
citations are unaffected: each is the page that carries the entry's own heading. A rendered page-by-page check would
be needed to compare rules, which this release does not do.

## Not found

- **Fill the Emptiness** (Spell, Void 4): no heading, mention or index entry in any of the 16 books. It keeps an
  empty citation.
- Name differences kept as the catalogue has them: **Enticing the Dance of Flame** is printed as "Enticing Dance of
  the Flame" (Core p.182); **Kyoketsu-shoge** as "Kyoketsu-shogi" and **Manrikigusari** as "Manrikikusari" (Core
  p.200); **Aiguchi** shares the "Aiguchi & Tanto" entry (Core p.201); **War Fan (Tessen)** is "War Fans" (Core p.205).
