Identity

Insight = (sum of all five Rings × 10) + total Skill Ranks + Insight Bonus. Insight Rank breakpoints: 0–149 Rank 1, 150–174 Rank 2, 175–199 Rank 3, then +25 per Rank. School Rank tracks Insight Rank automatically (and never decreases if Insight drops) — School Techniques for your current School unlock into the Techniques list below as you reach each Rank.

Skills

Roll = Skill Rank + Trait Rank, Keep = Trait Rank (adjust manually for High/Low or trait-only rolls). Pick a skill from the dropdown to add it with its Trait and Mastery notes already filled in, or use "+ Add Skill" for a custom one. Emphases: 1 at Rank 1, 2 at Rank 3, 3 at Rank 5, 4 at Rank 7, 5 at Rank 9 — 2 xp each, added from each skill's relevant list or as a custom entry.
